Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about admissions, exams, hostel, and college life.

What is the minimum attendance requirement for MITS semester exams?

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each registered course under MITS Autonomous Academic Regulations. Attendance shortage up to 10% (between 65% and 74.9%) can be condoned solely by the Director on valid medical hospitalization grounds or certified state/national event representation. Below 65% results in strict detention.

How does B.Tech admission counseling work at MITS Gwalior?

Admissions are conducted through MP DTE Online Counseling (dte.mponline.gov.in) based on JEE Main All India CRL Rank. 90% seats are reserved for MP State Domicile candidates, 10% for All India Quota, and 5% supernumerary seats under Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW). Vacant seats after centralized rounds are filled via on-campus College Level Counseling (CLC).

What are the hostel curfew timings and facilities at MITS?

MITS has 4 on-campus Boys Hostels (No. 1, 2, 3, 4) and a dedicated Girls Hostel complex. The campus curfew time is strictly 9:30 PM, monitored through biometric scanning. The complex includes student dining mess, 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, and recreation rooms.

What is the examination evaluation and grading system at MITS?

MITS adopts the 10-Point Absolute Grading Scale (O=10, A+=9, A=8, B+=7, B=6, C=5, P=4, F=0). Evaluation is divided into 30% Continuous Internal Assessment (2 Mid-Sem Tests + Quizzes/Assignments) and 70% End-Semester Autonomous Examination.

What are the library timings and digital resource access?

The Central Library operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on all academic working days. It houses 1,00,000+ print volumes, with institutional subscriptions to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and DELNET digital journals.

How can I contact the IT Department or college authorities?

The Department of Information Technology is headed by Dr. Punit Kumar Johari (HOD) in the IT & CSE Complex. General college contact: 0751-2409300 / director@mitsgwalior.in. Address: Race Course Road, Gola Ka Mandir, Gwalior – 474005.
